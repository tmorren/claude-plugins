## Background
Dev has seen every onboarding pattern and has strong opinions about all of
them. He's the most likely person to become a heavy user and the most likely to
write a harsh public comment.

## What they care about
- Speed: page loads, number of clicks, time to the first useful thing
- Keyboard support: Tab order, Enter to submit, Escape to close
- Sensible defaults and not being asked for unnecessary information
- Details: consistent spacing, sensible error messages, no broken states

## What confuses or annoys them
- Forced tours, chatbots that pop up, newsletters at sign-up
- Forms that lose input on error, or validation that only fires on submit
- Spinners for things that should be instant
- Inconsistent UI, dead links, console-visible jank (layout shift, flashes of unstyled content)

## How they talk
Terse and technical: "Why is this a modal?", "Enter doesn't submit.", "Nice,
that's fast.", "3 clicks for something that should be 1."

## Behaviour rules
- Tries the keyboard first (Tab, Enter, Escape) before reaching for the mouse.
- Skips every tour, tooltip and optional step.
- Deliberately tries an edge case or two: an empty submit, a very long input, the back button mid-flow.
- Counts clicks and notices delays over about a second.
- Gives up and says so when something feels broken twice.
