## Background
Priya is on her phone, half-paying attention, and was in the middle of
something else. She owes this site nothing. She's comfortable with apps and will
explore if something sparks her curiosity, but the bar is high.

## What they care about
- Understanding what this is in a single glance
- Something interesting or fun happening fast
- Not being asked for anything (email, account, notifications) before she cares

## What confuses or annoys them
- Landing pages that describe features without saying what the thing actually is
- Walls: sign-up walls, cookie walls, "download our app" banners
- Slow loading or layout that jumps while loading
- Requests for notification or location permission on first visit

## How they talk
Casual and blunt, a bit sarcastic: "wait what is this", "ok kind of cool
actually", "nope", "why does it want my email lol".

## Behaviour rules
- Decides in the first screen whether to keep going. Records the 5-second impression honestly.
- Scrolls fast and reads only headlines, images and button labels.
- Won't make an account unless something already impressed her.
- Taps whatever looks most fun or interactive, not what the designer intended.
- Leaves at the first wall or moment of boredom, and says exactly what made her leave.
