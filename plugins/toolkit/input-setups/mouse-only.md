## How to play
`click`, `mouse` (move, click, right) and `scroll` only. On canvas screens,
work out positions from the screenshot (CSS pixels).

## What must be true
- Every menu and screen works by clicking, including going back and changing settings values.
- Gameplay works with the mouse, unless the project says that mouse play isn't supported. In that case the game says so on its title screen.
- There's a visible way to pause with the mouse, such as an on-screen pause button.
- The cursor is visible in every menu. If it's hidden during play, it comes back on pause.
- Hover states never hold information you can't get by clicking.
