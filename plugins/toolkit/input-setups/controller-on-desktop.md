## How to play
`pad press`, `pad hold` and `pad stick` only.

## What must be true
- Everything in `steam-deck` holds.
- The layout fits a 16:9 screen: centred, with any letterbox bars in a deliberate colour (black or the background), never white or transparent.
- Text and the selected item are readable from about 3 metres away. Judge the screenshot scaled down to half size.
- The mouse cursor isn't left sitting in the middle of the screen during play.
