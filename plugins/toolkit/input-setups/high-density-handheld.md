## How to play
Same as `steam-deck`: `pad press`, `pad hold` and `pad stick` only.

## What must be true
- Everything in `steam-deck` holds.
- At 2x density, text, lines and icons are crisp. Zoom into the screenshot: blurry text or soft sprites mean something was drawn at 1x and scaled up.
- Nothing is clipped, misaligned or drawn at the wrong scale compared with the 1x screenshots.
