name: Buy or use a library
summary: Use an existing library, managed service or platform feature instead of building it.
---

## Brief
Find the best existing library, managed service or built-in platform feature
(of the framework, database or host the project already uses) that covers
this, and propose integrating it. Compare two or three candidates on fit,
maintenance health, licence, cost at the project's scale and lock-in. Check
the latest versions and pricing at their primary sources, not from memory, and
say what you couldn't verify.

## What it optimises for
Getting proven, maintained functionality without owning it.

## Blind spots
Integration and customisation costs, ongoing fees, vendor lock-in, and data
leaving the project's control. Check how hard it would be to switch away
later, and whether it handles the project's edge cases.
