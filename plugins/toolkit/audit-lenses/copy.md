## Checks
- Do buttons say what they do ("Save recipe", not "Submit" or "OK")?
- Are the same things called the same name everywhere, matching the rest of the app and any glossary?
- Do error messages say what went wrong and how to fix it, without blaming the user or showing codes?
- Is there jargon or internal naming a typical user wouldn't know?
- Are confirmations for destructive actions specific about what will be lost?
- Is the tone consistent with the rest of the product?

## How to check
Go through every screen and state of the feature and collect the visible text
from each snapshot, including errors (trigger them) and empty states. Compare
names against `GLOSSARY.md` or the rest of the app.

## What good looks like
A new user could read any screen aloud and understand exactly what will happen
when they press each button.
