---
id: discoverability
name: Discoverability
summary: Users who would benefit from the feature can find it and understand what it does.
needs: browser
---

## Checks
- Starting from the app's home screen, how many steps does it take to reach the feature, and is the path obvious?
- Is it named with words users would use, not internal or technical names?
- Is it reachable from the places where users would need it (related screens, empty states, search, menus)?
- Does its first use explain what it does, without a tour that has to be dismissed?
- Are its important options visible, or hidden behind icons with no labels?

## How to check
Navigate to the feature from the home screen as if you didn't know where it
was, and note each step. Compare the labels you pass with the feature's own
name. Read the navigation and routing code for other entry points.

## What good looks like
Someone who needs it finds it in three steps or fewer, from a place they were
already in, under a name they'd have guessed.
